I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in the [Department of Economics](https://www.up.ac.za/economics) at the [University of Pretoria](https://www.up.ac.za), South Africa. Before joining UP, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at [SOAS University of London](https://www.soas.ac.uk/about/colleges/college-development-economics-and-finance/department-economics) and a Research Fellow at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/environment-energy-resources/sustainable/research-institute-sustainable-resources/econometric-assessment). I have also held positions as an Africa Fellow at the [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/afr/brief/world-bank-group-africa-fellowship-program) and a PhD Intern at the [International Monetary Fund](https://www.imf.org/en/about/recruitment/working-at-the-imf/fund-internship-program).

My [**research**](/myresearch/) focuses on sustainable economic development at the intersection of <span style="color:#0f766e">**international trade**</span>, <span style="color:#0f766e">**environmental economics**</span>, and <span style="color:#0f766e">**development economics**</span>.
I study how trade interventions, resource endowments and the energy transition shape investment, domestic productive capabilities and structural transformation, with a particular focus on Africa.
