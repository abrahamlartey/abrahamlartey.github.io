I translate economic research into actionable evidence for governments and international organisations, focusing on energy, climate policy, industrial development, trade, and the transition to a low-carbon economy.

## Selected Policy Projects

### Energy and Industrial Development

- [**Green Ammonia for Low-Carbon Fertiliser Production: Cases Studies in North Africa**](https://hydrogen.unido.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/fertiliser-Report-May2026.pdf)  
  Analysis of the opportunities, constraints, and policy priorities for developing low-carbon ammonia and fertiliser value chains in Africa.

- [**Clean Hydrogen Skills Assessment Methodology**](https://hydrogen.unido.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Skills-report-1901_0.pdf) 
  Development of a methodology for identifying the workforce capabilities and skills required across the clean-hydrogen value chain.

- [**Energy Subsidy Reform in Action: Long-Run Impacts of Energy Prices on Consumer Spending**](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/19b87642-64c5-40f8-819a-61f9d92bc7f2/content)  
  Research examining how households adjust their spending to changes in energy prices and the implications for energy subsidy reform and carbon-pricing policy.

### Climate Policy and Programme Evaluation

- [**Evaluation of the UK Emissions Trading Scheme: Phase 1**](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/evaluation-of-the-uk-emissions-trading-scheme-phase-1)  
  Evaluation of the implementation and early outcomes of the United Kingdom’s principal carbon-pricing mechanism.

- [**Green Home Finance Innovation Fund: Impact Evaluation**](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68404b3d8dd459f8c947b40e/green-home-finance-innovation-fund-impact-report.pdf) 
  Assessment of innovative green-finance models designed to support the development and piloting of novel green home finance products for the UK home retrofit market.

- [**Evaluation of Phase 1 of the Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme**](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/67d15d7ba6d78876a3fb09fa/psds-phase-1-impact-evaluation.pdf)  
  Evaluation of a major public investment programme supporting energy efficiency and decarbonisation across public-sector buildings.

- [**Evaluation of the Green Homes Grant Voucher Scheme**](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/65427a221f1a600010360c16/ghgv-phase-3-final-outcome-evaluation-report.pdf)
  Assessment of the scheme’s effects on households, participating firms, residential energy efficiency, and value for money.
